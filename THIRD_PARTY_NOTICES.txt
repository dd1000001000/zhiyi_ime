rime-ice dictionary data
------------------------

Upstream: https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice
License: GNU General Public License v3.0 only (GPL-3.0-only)

The Pinyin dictionary data distributed with 知意输入法 is derived from the
rime-ice Chinese dictionaries and transformed into CxxIME's SQLite and runtime
binary formats. The English word list (data/english.words.tsv) is derived from
the rime-ice English dictionaries (en_dicts/en.dict.yaml and en_ext.dict.yaml)
by data/tools/build_english_dictionary.py, which ranks the words with wordfreq. The complete license text is distributed in
licenses/rime-ice-GPL-3.0.txt. The corresponding dictionary source and
transformation scripts are available in the source distribution under data/
and data/tools/ (originally https://github.com/deanxyuan/cxx-ime).

Unicode 17.0 classification data
--------------------------------

Upstream: https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/
License: Unicode-3.0

Unmodified upstream inputs are included in data/unicode/17.0/ for offline
regeneration; see data/unicode/README.md for their official download URLs.
data/tools/dict_builder/emoji_classification.json is derived from emoji-test.txt,
emoji-data.txt and UnicodeData.txt. Source URLs and SHA-256 values are recorded
in the generated file. The independent data/symbol_catalog.json uses selected
Unicode codepoints and ranges. Its dictionary-derived contents retain the
rime-wubi86-jidian attribution above.
The classification data is used offline; shared/src/symbol_ranges.inc derives
compact property ranges for personal vocabulary and learning admission.

Laya decision model
-------------------

Upstream: https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya
License: Apache License 2.0

The model in models/laya/ (laya.int8g.onnx, tokenizer.json, rl_agent_config.json)
is the multilingual Laya checkpoint fine-tuned with LoRA on Chinese pinyin and
English completion data, exported to ONNX and quantized to int8.
