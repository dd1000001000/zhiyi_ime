- Dictionary fix: about 34,000 words had wrong syllables (西安 read as one syllable, 办公室 split as bang-o-n-g-shi), so initials and sentence building could not find them; they are now split by each character's reading, so `bgs` → 办公室, `yxgs` → 有限公司, `xa` → 西安 and `hg` → 韩国 work
- Larger dictionary: common words that had been dropped from rime-ice (改代码, 微信群) and about 700,000 three- and four-character words (西安站, 高铁站) are back; the pinyin dictionary grows from about 400,000 to about 1.46 million entries
- Candidate order follows Rime: words matching every syllable come first, then by frequency; `qianm` gives 前面 rather than 阡陌, `jde` gives 觉得, and a complete `ni` no longer offers 年 first
- Sentence building follows Rime: fewer and more common words win, and complete syllables are no longer split into sentences such as 你看洗啊
- The context model is retrained on the new candidates; first-candidate accuracy on the IME's real candidates rises from 73.8% to 76.0% (unfinished last syllable 55.0% → 61.8%, mixed initials 45.5% → 58.5%)
- The server uses about 90 MB more memory
