- Common words are no longer lost when initials follow full pinyin: `womsh` gives 我们是, `zhegsh` gives 这个是 / 这个事, `tabsh` gives 他不是, `zhebsh` gives 这不是 (syllable paths are now enumerated most common first instead of alphabetically under a cap)
- Erhua: an r after a syllable reads as 儿, so `nar` gives 哪儿 / 那儿, `zher` 这儿, `yihuir` 一会儿, `huar` 花儿, `wanr` 玩儿
- Rare characters no longer fill the first page: single syllables such as `de`, `ne`, `nv` show common characters and words first, and characters almost never used go last
- The last syllable is always completed: `buzhida` gives 不知道, `xianggan` 香港, `huanyin` 欢迎
- At most three predicted longer words per input: `bandaoti` no longer shows a whole page of 半导体… phrases
