- New learning mode: the candidate window shows what each candidate means in another language, so you pick up a language while typing. Chinese candidates can be translated into English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish or Russian, English candidates into Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish or Russian; up to three common senses per word with English part-of-speech labels (n. v. adj. …), at 80% of the candidate font; words with several readings are translated by reading (行 háng → row / bank)
- New Learning page in the settings: choose the languages (languages not downloaded are grayed out) with a live preview; language packs can be downloaded, checked for updates and removed, downloads are verified by signature and SHA-256, and packs take effect as soon as they are installed or removed. The installer includes Chinese → English and English → Chinese; the other 12 are downloaded when needed
- Version 1 of the language packs covers common words only (the 20,000 most frequent Chinese words, the 8,000 most frequent English words); the translations are generated by large language models and may contain occasional mistakes
- The candidate window is now vertical by default; a new Candidates option on the General page switches back to horizontal (translations are shown in the vertical layout only)
- The settings program has a new card-based look: icons in the page list, settings grouped on each page, redesigned buttons and menus, in light and dark
- New Start when I sign in option on the General page: when off, the background service starts the first time you switch to Zhiyi IME instead of at sign-in
- New Exit item in the right-click menu of the taskbar indicator: closes the background service, which starts again the next time you switch to Zhiyi IME
- Learned words no longer show a small dot in the candidate window
