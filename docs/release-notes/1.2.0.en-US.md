- Learning mode can now translate offline: phrases, rare words and short sentences the language packs lack are translated on the graphics card by the Tencent Hy-MT2 1.8B model, downloaded on the Learning page (about 1.2 GB). A page is translated at once; candidates show first and their translations follow (about 0.1 s per page on an RTX 5070 Ti). It works offline and unloads after a minute unused. Needs a dedicated graphics card with more than 2 GB of memory; you choose which card
- New "Data location" on the General page: the user dictionary, learning records, language packs, logs, model caches and downloaded models move together to another drive; the copy is checked before the switch and the old data on drive C is deleted
- The context recommendation model no longer runs when there is no text before the caret; candidates keep their order
- Fixed: on computers with a dedicated graphics card, "Candidates per page" and "Interface language" at the bottom of the General page were cut off; settings pages now scroll
