- New: **voice input** (Settings → Voice input). Press the shortcut and speak; press it again, or click stop on the voice bar at the bottom of the screen, and what you said goes into the input box as text. Speech is recognized on the computer; recordings are never kept or sent anywhere. The bar's waves move while someone speaks; typing pauses while you speak and while the text is recognized, and Esc or leaving the input box drops the recording. The text is underlined: Backspace or Esc deletes it, Enter keeps it (no new line, so a chat message is not sent), and typing on keeps it too. Mandarin, English, Cantonese and Chinese mixed with English; numbers and dates as digits (can be turned off). The bar says so when it heard no speech, finds no microphone or may not use it. The shortcut is set on the Keys page (a new fifth switch key: Shift or Ctrl tapped alone, or a combination; none by default). The voice model (SenseVoice-Small, about 240 MB) is downloaded on the Voice input page when wanted and runs on the CPU or a graphics card; choosing a card first compares its speed with the CPU's. See [Voice input](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/blob/main/docs/voice-input.md)
- New: **word lists** (Settings → Word lists). Import Sogou `.scel` cell dictionaries, Rime `.dict.yaml` dictionaries, pinyin text exports and English word lists, up to 64 Chinese and 64 English lists, each turned on or off on its own. Imported words appear among the candidates and in whole sentences like the system's words; words of a pinyin list can be typed in shuangpin, initials and Wubi too, words of a Wubi list in pinyin
- English word completion: the typed text stays first, with up to two pages of completions and spelling corrections that turn with PageUp / PageDown and the page keys set in Settings; Tab / Shift+Tab move the highlight within the page and wrap around
- The recommendation model's "Run on" now lists discrete cards with 1 GB of their own memory or more (2 GB before; the model takes about 500 MB on a card), and integrated graphics are no longer listed. The memory each of the three models (recommendation, offline translation, voice) asks of a card can be changed in the config file (`laya.min_gpu_memory_mb`, `learning.translator_min_gpu_memory_mb`, `voice.min_gpu_memory_mb`)
- Programs open before the update need to be opened again to use voice input (the voice bar says so)
