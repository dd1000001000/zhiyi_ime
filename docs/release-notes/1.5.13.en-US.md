- The voice input shortcut can be pressed twice or held (Settings > Keys, beside the voice shortcut, #25): pressed twice, it starts and stops recording, and a 2-second pause without speech also ends it and puts the text in at once (`voice.auto_stop_ms`); held, you speak while holding it and release to finish
- All shortcuts tell a tap from a hold the same way (`shortcuts.hold_ms`, 400 ms by default); pressing another shortcut while holding one counts as releasing the first; the other switch keys work while recording
- With voice input off, the voice shortcut is no longer taken by the IME and shows no message (#29)
- Words of languages written with spaces, such as English: picked with a number, the mouse or Enter they get a space after them; committed with Space or a punctuation mark they get just that mark (Settings > General > "Add a space automatically", on by default, #28). English candidates in Chinese and translations typed in learning mode follow the same rule, and no space is added before a translation any more
