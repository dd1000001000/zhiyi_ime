- Emoji (#31): among pinyin candidates, a word with an emoji is followed by it, e.g. typing `xiao` shows 😄 ☺️ after 笑; at most 3 a page, the first candidate is always a word, and picking an emoji teaches nothing (Settings > General > "Show emoji among pinyin candidates", on by default). `\bq` browses all 1601 emoji; the personal dictionary can hold emoji, e.g. `dz` for 👍; the candidate window shows emoji in color
- With Chinese punctuation on, a period typed right after a number is a decimal point, so `3.14` types as is (#32); a comma after a number is still "，"
- Settings > General has a new "English input": English mode can give word completions or letters only (each key goes straight to the program, for terminals and vim). It is the same setting as the input style key while typing and the tray menu, and takes effect when applied. "Spelling correction" and "Add a space automatically" now share a row
- The `\` symbol categories are ordered by use: emoji, common symbols and punctuation first, radicals last; common symbols start with stars, shapes, ※ and ✓
