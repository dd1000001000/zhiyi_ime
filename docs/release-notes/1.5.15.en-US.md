- An English word among Chinese candidates no longer gets a space, like a Chinese candidate: picked with Space, a number or the mouse, it goes alone. "Add a space automatically" now applies to English mode and learning-mode translations only
- The explanations on the Settings pages are shorter, in Chinese and English; in English, options and boxes no longer run past the edge of their card
- Fixed an occasional crash when switching the interface language in Settings
- In games that draw their own text box, such as Age of Empires II, the candidate window no longer goes to the bottom-right corner of the screen; it follows the pinyin being typed (#24)
- Fixed an occasional "could not confirm that the Zhiyi background service has stopped" error when installing or updating: the seconds while the service exits and releases the graphics card no longer count as a failure
