- New suggestion model: long pinyin is now written by a decoder a character at a time, each character given the context, all the typed pinyin and the characters already written, comparing several paths over the dictionary's words; its composition part was retrained on long text with real context. Long input typed in one go comes out right more often: full pinyin 60.7% to 61.4% (11-16 characters 53.2% to 55.4%), first syllables abbreviated 16.2% to 18.1%; the first candidate on real IME pages 78.1% to 78.5%, homophones and English completion unchanged. Word choice on short input is about 4 ms faster per page on the CPU
