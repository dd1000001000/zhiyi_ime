- Fixed `xian` not offering 西安: when the pinyin reads as one syllable and also splits into two (`xian` 西安, `tian` 提案, `jie` 饥饿, `qie` 企鹅, `piao` 皮袄), the two-syllable words came after every single character (西安 was 25th); as in rime-ice, up to two such longer words now go fourth, and the context model may move them further (after 我明天去, `xian` puts 西安 first)
- The apostrophe `'` separates syllables while typing pinyin (`xi'an` 西安, `pi'ao` 皮袄, `fang'an` 方案); it used to commit the first candidate with a quotation mark (西‘). When not typing pinyin it is still a quotation mark. With apostrophes typed, learned words must break there too: 显示 learned for `xianshi` no longer answers `xi'anshi`
