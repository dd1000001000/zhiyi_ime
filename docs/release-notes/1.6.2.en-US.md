- Custom phrases (#40): Settings > Phrases; a code brings up a prepared phrase at the place you choose (`dh` for a phone number). The file uses Sogou's `phrases.ini` format; phrases can be added, edited, imported (Sogou, Rime), exported and cleared, with Sogou's date variables (`#$year` …). Pinyin read as 日期 / 时间 / 星期 (`rq`, `sj`, `shijian`, `xq` …) offers the current date, time and weekday, also the last three groups of the `/` menu. Phrases chosen are not learned, not used as context and not collected
- U mode and V mode (#41), as in Microsoft Pinyin, switched on the new Settings > Advanced page: `v` first types numbers in Chinese, amounts in capitals, dates (lunar date and weekday too), times, formulas and character codes (`v123` 一百二十三 / 壹佰贰拾叁, `v1+2*3` 7), chosen with a, b, c; `u` first finds a character by its strokes (h s p n z) or the readings of its parts, showing its readings, and `uu` plus an abbreviation types symbols. Shift+U / Shift+V in shuangpin and Wubi
- Alt as a switch key (#42): the Chinese/English, punctuation, full/half width, input style and voice input keys can each be a tapped Alt, left, right or either; a tapped Alt no longer opens the application's menu bar
- Fixed Shift+letter shortcuts in Aegisub and other programs being taken as pinyin (#39)
- An English word among Chinese candidates stays second (an emoji after the first word used to push it to third or fourth); words you reordered by hand are no longer moved by the context model
