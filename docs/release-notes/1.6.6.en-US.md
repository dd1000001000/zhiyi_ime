- The recommendation model is now the slim graph of ML-Flash-1103: the same model without retraining, with a 4-bit token table and 8-bit attention projections in the choice head; the model file in the installer goes from 78 to 53 MB and takes about 20 MB less memory, at the same speed. End to end in the IME (CPU, quick inputs off, the tests of 1.6.5): Chinese short 88.8 → 88.7, long 76.3 → 76.1, English 90.1 → 89.6
- Voice input has a choice of model (Settings > Voice input > Model): SenseVoice-Small as before, or the new Qwen3-ASR-0.6B (about 680 MB, on the CPU). Qwen3-ASR is more accurate (error rates on 200 rows of each test set: AISHELL-1 4.12 → 2.77, FLEURS Mandarin 7.87 → 6.58, LibriSpeech 3.62 → 2.15, FLEURS English 9.42 → 5.77); the text usually comes 0.5 to 1 s after you stop
- With Qwen3-ASR the text appears while you speak: every 2 s what was said so far is recognized and shown on the voice bar; when you stop, the whole recording is recognized once more and goes into the text box
