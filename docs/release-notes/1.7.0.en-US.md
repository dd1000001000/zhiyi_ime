- New: next-word suggestions. After a word is committed (a candidate picked in Chinese, or a word finished with Space with English input "Words"), the words most likely to come next are listed at the caret, up to one page (the candidates-per-page setting); words the model is unsure of are left out. Turn it on or off in Settings > General > Recommendations > "Show next words after a word" (on by default). See [Next words](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/blob/main/docs/next-words.md)
  - Main-row digits pick a word, and the next suggestions follow; numpad digits still type digits
  - In learning mode the suggestions show translations too, Ctrl+digit types the translation, and suggestions follow it as well (swapped when "Number keys type the translation" is on)
  - Esc closes the suggestions; any other key, a click elsewhere in the document, or moving to another input box or program closes them at once, and the key works as usual
  - They are computed in the background (on the CPU about 26 ms for Chinese, 14 ms for English), so typing is not slowed down; Chinese suggestions are ranked together with the word n-gram
  - On a 1000-sentence test set, the next word is among the first 10 suggestions 29.0% of the time in Chinese and 46.8% in English
- The recommended model is now ml-flash-next-preview-1114: ML-Flash-1103 with a continuation decoder added; word choice and composition stay as before (end to end in the IME, CPU, quick inputs off: Chinese short 88.7 → 88.5, long 76.1 → 76.1, English 89.6 → 90.7). The installer grows from 135 to 145 MB
- Fixed single-key shortcuts in English mode ([#46](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/issues/46)): with English input set to "Letters only", the IME used to type letters itself, against what the setting says, so the program never got the key, and shortcuts such as Photoshop's B for the brush did nothing on the canvas; now letter keys go straight to the program, as with no IME, and still type letters in text fields. Full-width letters are still typed by the IME. In Chinese and with English input "Words" a letter still starts typing; to use single-key shortcuts in Photoshop, switch to English with "Letters only"
- The offline translator for learning mode is now translation model V5 (shown as v5 in Settings); update it in Settings > Learning
- After the update, programs that were already open keep the old IME component; reopen them to get the suggestions
