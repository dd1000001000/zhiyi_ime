- New "My words" list ([#47](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/issues/47)): add Chinese words and English words or phrases in Settings > Word lists > Add words…, without importing a whole dictionary. One word a line, up to 100 lines at a time, no total limit. See the [settings guide](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/blob/main/docs/settings-guide.md)
  - Every line is previewed before adding: new, already in the dictionary, already in My words, repeated, or why it cannot be added
  - The reading of a Chinese word is guessed; when it cannot be guessed or is unusual, write the pinyin after a space, such as `银行家 yin hang jia`
  - The added words are ranked by the IME like any other word, with no fixed position, and move up as you use them
  - The My words row can be turned off, edited (paged search, change a reading, remove selected words) or removed; restoring a backup merges it with the words you have
- Fixed the ü final in shuangpin ([#49](https://github.com/dd1000001000/zhiyi_ime/issues/49)): after j/q/x/y (与, 句, 去, 需), all five shuangpin schemes accept both the u key and the ü key, so in Xiaohe both `yv` and `yu` type 与. The keys that worked before give the same results
- Fixed the candidate window becoming an empty small bar in every program, until the IME restarted, after another program (such as some screen capture or recording tools) minimized it; it now comes back on the next key
- After the update, programs that were already open keep the old IME component; reopen them for the fixes to take effect
